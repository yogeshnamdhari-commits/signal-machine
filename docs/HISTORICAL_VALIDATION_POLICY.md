# Historical Validation Policy

Dated audit, validation, readiness, and forward-test documents are immutable research records. Their results describe the repository state, configuration, data, and tests available at the time they were produced.

They are **not** current deployment certification.

A current certification must be generated from executable evidence tied to the exact source commit and effective configuration fingerprint. The authoritative current state is represented by the machine-readable certification artifact and `docs/VALIDATION_STATUS.md`.

No historical report can transition a repository to `LIVE_ELIGIBLE` merely by existing in the tree.
