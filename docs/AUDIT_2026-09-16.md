## Signal authenticity contract

The executable `Signal` field has only three states: `BUY`, `SELL`, and `NO_SIGNAL`. A dashboard is not allowed to infer a trade signal by voting across OI, CVD, Flow, imbalance, volume, or other displayed factors.

Every factor is separately labelled with a directional state and data-quality state. Raw values remain raw; contextual values such as sweep price, FVG price, regime confidence, and liquidation risk are not treated as standalone trading votes.

## Missing values

A missing field is not an implicit zero and not an implicit neutral vote. The dashboard explicitly renders `UNAVAILABLE`, `STALE`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. A fresh bridge snapshot is required before a canonical executable signal is displayed as current.

## Research integrity

The project must not convert an engineering pass into a profitability claim. Current documentation explicitly separates `ENGINEERING_VALID`, `RESEARCH_VALIDATED`, and `LIVE_ELIGIBLE`. The final state cannot be `LIVE_ELIGIBLE` without current reproducible statistical evidence.

A current research run still has to be performed on valid historical/forward data with realistic fees, spread, slippage, funding, temporal separation, regime coverage, and rejected-signal outcome attribution. Until that evidence passes the research gate, live execution remains blocked.
